## What Agents Do

Workflow agents for Hong Kong legal practice:

- **Contract triage** — Inbound agreements sorted against your playbook before lawyer time is spent
- **First-pass review** — Flagged drafts with citations where possible, ready for professional judgment
- **Deadline monitoring** — Renewals, filings, and matter dates tracked with escalation drafts when decisions are needed

Agents connect to existing systems — iManage, NetDocuments, Ironclad, SharePoint, Google Drive, and others. Documents stay in your folders; we do not train on your client or business data.
