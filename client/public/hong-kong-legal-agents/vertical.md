## Agent Roles

Each agent is placed into a defined role — narrow job description, hard boundaries, measured KPIs. Like a hire, not a SaaS tool.

### Contract Triage Specialist

- **Role:** Sort inbound contracts and NDAs against your playbook before lawyer time is spent
- **Pain:** Agreements pile up faster than lawyers can review them
- **KPI:** Inbound contracts sorted, triage accuracy rate, time-to-first-review

### First-Pass Review Specialist

- **Role:** Flag clauses, draft summaries, and surface citations — ready for lawyer judgment
- **Pain:** Rebuilding the same review memos from scratch for every matter
- **KPI:** Review turnaround time, citation accuracy, matter coverage

### Deadline Operations Specialist

- **Role:** Track renewals, filings, and matter dates — draft escalation reminders when decisions are needed
- **Pain:** Deadlines nobody owns end to end
- **KPI:** Deadline compliance rate, missed deadlines, escalation timeliness

Agents connect to existing systems — iManage, NetDocuments, Ironclad, SharePoint, Google Drive, and others. Documents stay in your folders; we do not train on your client or business data.
